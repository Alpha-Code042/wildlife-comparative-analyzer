Interpreting the Results

YOLO detection results and traditional feature-extraction results measure different capabilities. SIFT keypoint counts, HOG feature-vector lengths and edge-pixel percentages are not wildlife detection accuracy measurements.

Processing speed depends on video resolution, hardware, model inference time and the algorithms being evaluated. A fair benchmark should use the same input video and consistent evaluation conditions.
