How to interpret these results

YOLO produces wildlife detections for classes supported by the configured model. The classical OpenCV baseline produces grayscale and edge images without species classification.

SIFT extracts local keypoints and descriptors, while HOG represents gradient patterns numerically. Their feature counts and vector lengths are not wildlife detection accuracy measurements.

Processing times are measurements from this run. They can vary with hardware, image size, and model-loading overhead. They should not be treated as a controlled benchmark yet.

Edge-pixel percentage is an image statistic, not detection accuracy. Accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score require suitable ground-truth annotations and a consistent evaluation procedure.
